# Requirements and discovery gates

## Confirmed for this local increment

1. Provide a tangible OHWorks-specific pilot experience inside the existing LIMS BOX repository.
2. Keep all displayed data synthetic and conspicuously labelled.
3. Demonstrate accession, analyzer ingestion, technical review, and release as separate states.
4. Keep analyzer ingestion separate from result release and require a human authorization decision.
5. Demonstrate personnel competence and authorization evidence.
6. Preserve auditability for parser/mapping versions, quarantine, review, and release decisions.
7. Treat the reported 30,000–40,000 annual samples as a planning range, not a verified capacity requirement.
8. Leave production, identity, hosting, customer data, communication, and spend stopped behind named gates.

## Discovery required before an interface adapter

- Exact analyzer make/model, software and firmware versions.
- ASTM, HL7, file, API, middleware, serial, TCP, or other transport.
- Message framing, encoding, acknowledgement, retry, timeout, and sequence behavior.
- Order/result correlation fields and specimen identifier rules.
- Example messages with sensitive content removed or generated by a simulator.
- Test, component, unit, qualitative result, flag, and instrument-error mapping.
- Duplicate, amended, corrected, cancelled, rerun, and partial-result behavior.

## Discovery required before real users or data

- Clinical intended use and governing quality/accreditation scope.
- Data controller/processor roles, approved regions, retention, deletion, backup, recovery, and incident process.
- Identity provider, MFA, role design, joiner/mover/leaver process, and privileged access review.
- Pilot users, authorization matrix, training evidence, and acceptance owner.
- One pilot workflow, baseline, measurable outcomes, entry/exit criteria, and rollback trigger.

## Acceptance boundary

This increment may prove software behavior using synthetic fixtures. It cannot prove customer workflow fit, interface compatibility, legal or regulatory conformity, clinical validation, production security, or operational readiness.
