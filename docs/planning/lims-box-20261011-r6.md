## J1 — Do not demand a repeat at the exact decimal carryover limit

- **Source:** `lib/ohworks-carryover.ts:306` (comparison at line 307).
- **Test:** `tests/ohworks/carryover.test.ts`.
- **Verified defect:** preceding value `0.1`, factor `0.1`, repeat threshold `0.01`, high threshold `0.1`, next value `0.001` produces contribution `0.010000000000000002` and `must_repeat`. The declared inclusive threshold and existing exact-boundary test require `at_risk` for mathematical equality.
- **User impact:** the analytical-run assessment can tell the operator to repeat a result that is within the declared threshold.
- **Bounded repair:** handle multiplication roundoff at the comparison using magnitude-scaled tolerance; preserve the contribution evidence and retain rejection of genuine excesses. Do not introduce a fixed absolute epsilon that swallows small thresholds.
- **Acceptance:** the decimal equality above is `at_risk`; a clearly higher contribution remains `must_repeat`, including small-magnitude cases; wash/reset and existing cases remain unchanged.
