As a graduate Teaching Assistant (TA) I participated in the instruction, advising, and evaluation of undergraduates. Most of my teaching experiences where for laboratory classes, which gave me the opportunity to encounter a high amount of one on one personal contact with my students. It is through this personal contact that allowed me to assess my students individually based on there personal needs. For this reason I found myself responsible for the success and failure my students. I was able learn what factors contributed to the success and failure of my students and how to adjust my teachings in a way that would lead to a greater percent of successful students. 
My experience as a TA allowed me to provide a personal impact on students, whether it was through promoting personal motivation, mentoring, analysis of student’s needs and presenting difficult subject matter in ways that students could more easily understand and learn. 
Being a TA has taught me the art of good teaching, stimulated my interest in teaching and provided me with skills to teach effectively. It is these skills that I will continue to apply inside and outside of class.
