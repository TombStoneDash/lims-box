I was founder and President of the first ever chemistry club at my community college.
I designed the club in lieu of the fact that I had a deep passion for chemistry and wanted to spread this enthusiasm to the youth of the community.   I hoped to pass on my interest and motivate young students to stay in school and pursue a college degree in the field of science. I pursued this goal by developing visual chemistry experiments that would make a lasting impression in a child’s mind. With these experiments in hand, I would then invite local classes spanning first through eighth grade to view the demonstrations. While I viewed the children’s faces as I performed the demonstrations, I noticed that they had the same look on their faces that I have had while watching a physician at work. I hope that I have made the same lasting impressions on these children as the physician has made on me, and that this has given them the drive that I have.
