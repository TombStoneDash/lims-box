University link Medical Science Program / Mentor

After participating in the University Link Medical Science Program at UCSD, I returned that following summer and every summer thereafter to serve as a mentor for the new disadvantaged students (in the program) pursing a health professional career. As a mentor, I gave guidance to help the students on reaching their maximum potential to pursue a career in health care, assisted in their health disparity research posters, provided pre-medical advising and increased their understanding of population-based workforce needs.
Mentoring enabled me to practice my social and communication skills by interacting with students as well as re-instilling into me the specific aims of the program, reminding me of my goal to help increase both the care and quality of health care in a medically underserved community.

As an alumnus to the ULMSP I gave back to the program what I had got out of it.
The progam had such a positive renforcemtst to my goals I wanted to help in any way.
In addition to being a participant in this program I also served as a mentor to fellow generation of participants each summer instilling into them the same qualities I acquired.
