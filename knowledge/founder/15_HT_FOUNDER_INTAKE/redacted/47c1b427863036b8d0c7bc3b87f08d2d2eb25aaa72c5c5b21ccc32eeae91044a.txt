Born and raised in the Imperial Valley (El Centro), my grandfather told me stories about how great the Salton Sea was. As I grew older I only heard negative things about the Sea. Just 3 days ago I drove back from Palm Spring and decided to take the 111, a route I have not taken since I was a child, I was amazed how desolate but yet stunning it looked. As a chemist at a near by Geothermal plant I am optimistic that this great landmark will once again be a place to make many memories to share with my grandchildren.
