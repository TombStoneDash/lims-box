
Hi my name is John “Hudson” Taylor, most people call me Hud. 
I currently work as an analytical chemist; I like my job but do not love my job. What I love is chemistry (organic), biology and cooking. 
I was born and raised in a small town, El Centro CA, it was not till I moved away for college, UCSD, that I knew of any other beer besides “Bud Light”. Not until then did my pallet and love for beer develop. As a son of a farmer and grandson of a cattle man I only new of two beers, the one we drink (“Bud”) and the one we don’t (“Coors”). 
As my eyes have now been opened to a whole new world of beers, ones that I much admired. Returning to El Centro I realized that my own town is deprived of something great. Something, that I can see being a huge success, in this growing town. As a Chemist, Biologist, Chief and an inventor by nature. This has become my passion and I’m enthused about starting this new adventure to share with my family, friends, community and fellow deprived beer consumers.
